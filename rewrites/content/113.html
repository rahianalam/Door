Every guide on Dooroora is written to help you solve a real door problem safely and correctly. This page explains how we choose topics, research and write our content, check facts, keep articles current and correct mistakes, so you know exactly why you can trust what you read here.

Our Mission

Dooroora exists to answer every door question clearly: how to install it, fix it, secure it and choose the right product. We write for homeowners, renters, DIYers and tradespeople who want practical, accurate answers without the fluff.

Our Editorial Principles

  • Accuracy first. We base our guidance on manufacturer specifications, recognized standards and established trade practice.
  • Practical and specific. We give real measurements, part names, tools and step-by-step instructions, not vague advice.
  • Safety-aware. We flag risky jobs clearly and tell you when to call a licensed professional.
  • Independent. No brand pays for a recommendation, and our conclusions are our own.
  • Reader-focused. We answer the question you searched for at the top of the page, then go deeper.

How We Choose Topics

We cover the questions people actually ask about doors. Topics come from reader emails, search trends, common installation and repair problems, and gaps where existing information is unclear, outdated or wrong. Each topic must solve a real problem or help someone make a better decision.

How We Research and Write

Every guide goes through the same process:

  1. Research. We review manufacturer installation manuals and spec sheets, product documentation, and relevant standards such as ANSI/BHMA hardware grades, the International Residential Code (IRC), the National Electrical Code (NEC) and ADA guidance where they apply.
  2. Practical verification. We cross-check steps and measurements against established carpentry, locksmithing and installation practice to make sure the instructions work in real homes.
  3. Clear writing. We lead with the direct answer, then explain the why, the steps, common mistakes and troubleshooting. We use tables for comparisons and specifications so key facts are easy to find.
  4. Safety review. Any guide involving electricity, garage door springs, structural framing, fire-rated doors or egress is checked for clear safety warnings.

Fact-Checking

Before publishing, each guide is reviewed for:

  • Measurements, dimensions and tolerances
  • Product names, model numbers and features
  • Code and standard references
  • Prices and cost ranges, which are labeled with the year they apply to
  • Safety warnings and when-to-call-a-pro guidance

Where standards differ between countries, such as door handle heights or electrical rules in the US, UK and Australia, we say so and explain the difference.

Keeping Content Up to Date

Products change, codes are revised and prices move. We regularly review our guides, prioritizing those that cover fast-changing topics such as smart locks, smart doorbells and pricing. When we make a meaningful update, we change the "Updated" date shown on the article.

Corrections Policy

If we get something wrong, we fix it. When an error is reported or found:

  • We review the claim against primary sources.
  • We correct the article promptly if it is wrong.
  • For significant corrections affecting safety or key instructions, we note the correction on the page.

Spotted an error? Please tell us through our contact page and include the page link and what needs correcting.

Independence, Products and Advertising

  • We do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage or recommendations.
  • When we name brands or products, it is because they are relevant, widely available or a useful example, not because we were paid to include them.
  • If any page includes affiliate or sponsored links, we disclose it clearly on that page. These relationships never change our recommendations.
  • Advertising, if shown, is kept separate from editorial content.
