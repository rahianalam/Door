To install a lock on an interior door, choose a privacy knob or lever (push-button lock with an emergency release), check the door's backset and thickness, then fit the latch and handles through the existing 2-1/8" hole. On a pre-drilled door it takes about 15 minutes with a screwdriver. If the door has no hole, you drill one with a lock installation kit. If you need more privacy than a push-button lock, a keyed lever or a surface bolt are the next steps up.

Understanding Interior Lock Types

Privacy Locks

A privacy lock has a push button or turn button inside and a small emergency release on the outside. The release opens with a pin or a flat tool, so anyone locked in can be reached quickly. This is the standard choice for bathrooms and bedrooms.

Keyed Entry Locks

A keyed lever or knob locks with a key from outside and a button inside. Use it for an office, a lodger's room or storage. Keep a spare key somewhere accessible.

Interior Deadbolts and Surface Bolts

For stronger security on a storage room, a single-cylinder deadbolt adds a solid bolt. For simple inside-only privacy, a surface-mounted slide bolt screws onto the door face and frame.

No-Drill Options

Renters can use portable door locks, door wedges or over-the-door latches. See can you put a lock on a door without drilling. Before adding a lock to a bedroom in a rental, read can I legally put a lock on my bedroom door.

Measure Before You Buy

  • Backset: distance from the door edge to the center of the round hole, usually 2-3/8" on interior doors.
  • Door thickness: usually 1-3/8" for interior doors.
  • Bore hole: standard is 2-1/8".
  • Latch type: drive-in (round face) or faceplate (rectangular). Match what the door has to avoid chiseling.

Most new latches adjust between 2-3/8" and 2-3/4", so the backset is rarely a problem on standard doors.

How to Install a Privacy Lock on a Pre-Drilled Door

Tools: Phillips screwdriver. Time: about 15 minutes.

Step 1: Remove the Old Handle

Remove the two screws on the inside rose. If none are visible, press the small slot on the knob neck with a thin screwdriver, pull the knob off and pop the rose to reveal the screws. Slide both halves out, then unscrew and remove the latch.

Step 2: Install the New Latch

Set the latch to your backset, insert it with the angled side of the latch bolt facing the direction the door closes, and screw it in flush.

Step 3: Fit the Outside Handle

The side with the small emergency release hole goes on the outside (hallway side). Push its spindle and posts through the latch.

Step 4: Fit the Inside Handle

The push-button side goes inside the room. Line it up, insert the screws and tighten evenly until snug. Overtightening makes the handle stiff.

Step 5: Check the Strike

Close the door slowly. The latch should click into the strike plate. If it misses, move the strike slightly or file the opening.

Step 6: Test the Lock and Emergency Release

Lock the door from inside, then open it from outside using the emergency release tool. Make sure everyone in the home knows where the tool is kept.

How to Install a Lock on an Interior Door With No Hole

  1. Mark the height: handle centers are usually about 36" from the floor. Match other doors in the house.
  2. Use the template: fold it over the door edge and mark the face hole and edge hole for a 2-3/8" backset.
  3. Drill the 2-1/8" face hole with a hole saw, finishing from the other side to prevent splintering.
  4. Drill the 1" edge hole into the face hole, keeping the bit square.
  5. Mortise the latch plate if your latch has a rectangular faceplate.
  6. Install the lock using the steps above, then fit the strike plate on the frame.

Hollow-core door? Most interior hollow-core doors have a solid wood block only where the factory expected the handle. Drill there. Drilling elsewhere leaves nothing for the latch to grip.

Common Mistakes

  • Putting the release hole on the inside. The emergency release must face the hallway.
  • Latch upside down. The door will not latch when pushed closed.
  • Drilling a hollow-core door in the wrong place. There is no solid wood to hold the lock.
  • Locking a child's room from inside. Choose hardware with an easy emergency release, or skip the lock.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of lock goes on a bedroom door?

A privacy lever or knob is standard. Choose a keyed entry lever if you need to lock the room while you are away.

Can you put a lock on a hollow-core door?

Yes, at the factory-made handle location, which has solid blocking. Avoid adding deadbolts to hollow-core doors.

How do you open a locked privacy door from outside?

Push a pin or small flat screwdriver into the hole or slot in the outside handle and push or turn until the lock releases.

Can I install an interior lock myself?

Yes. On a pre-drilled door it is a 15-minute job with a screwdriver.

Can I put a deadbolt on an interior door?

Yes on a solid-core door. Use a single-cylinder model so the room can always be opened from inside.

The Bottom Line

For most interior doors, a privacy knob or lever is the right lock: it installs in minutes using the existing hole and can always be opened in an emergency.
