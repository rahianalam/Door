Yes, you can install a smart lock without drilling, as long as your door already has a standard deadbolt. Retrofit smart locks replace only the interior thumb turn and keep your existing exterior cylinder and keys, while "stick-on" smart locks attach over the thumb turn with adhesive and turn it for you. Both install in 10 to 20 minutes with a screwdriver or no tools at all. If your door has no deadbolt hole, there is no true no-drill smart deadbolt: you would need to drill one or use a renter-friendly portable lock instead.

The Three Types of No-Drill Smart Locks

1. Retrofit Smart Locks

A retrofit lock removes only the inside thumb turn of your current deadbolt and mounts a motorized unit in its place. The outside of your door looks the same and your keys still work. Examples include the August Wi-Fi Smart Lock and the Yale Approach. Because you keep the original bolt and cylinder, security is the same as your existing deadbolt, so it is worth checking that the deadbolt itself is good quality (Grade 2 or better).

2. Adhesive "Over the Thumb Turn" Smart Locks

These mount on top of your existing interior thumb turn with strong adhesive pads and rotate it with a motor. Nothing is removed. They suit renters and short-term setups. The trade-off is that they are bulkier and their grip on oddly shaped thumb turns can vary, so check the compatibility list before buying.

3. Full Smart Deadbolts Using Existing Holes

If your door already has standard deadbolt holes (2-1/8" face bore, 1" edge bore), any standard smart or keypad deadbolt installs without new drilling. You replace the whole deadbolt, inside and out. Our guide on how to install a keypad door lock covers this.

Before You Buy: Compatibility Checklist

  • Single-cylinder deadbolt: retrofit and adhesive locks need an interior thumb turn. Double-cylinder deadbolts (keyed both sides) are not compatible.
  • Smooth bolt action: turn your thumb turn by hand with the door closed. If you have to push, pull or lift the door to lock it, the motor will struggle. Fix alignment first.
  • Clearance: the smart unit is larger than a thumb turn. Check space near door trim, storm doors and interior handles.
  • Thumb turn shape: adhesive models list compatible shapes. Measure yours.
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi models connect directly; others need a bridge or a smart home hub. Check support for your ecosystem (Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, Matter).
  • Renting? Check your lease. Retrofit locks are reversible: you can reinstall the original thumb turn when you move out.

How to Install a Retrofit Smart Lock (No Drilling)

Tools: Phillips screwdriver. Time: 10 to 20 minutes.

Step 1: Test Your Deadbolt

Close the door and lock and unlock it with the thumb turn. It should move freely through its full throw. If it binds, adjust the strike plate before going further.

Step 2: Remove the Interior Thumb Turn

Unscrew the two screws holding the thumb turn. Hold the exterior cylinder in place with one hand so it does not fall out. Keep the original thumb turn and screws somewhere safe.

Step 3: Attach the Mounting Plate

Fit the smart lock's mounting plate using your existing deadbolt screws or the longer screws included. Make sure the cylinder tailpiece passes through the center opening. Many kits include adapters for different tailpiece shapes; use the one that fits snugly.

Step 4: Mount the Smart Lock

Line the smart unit up with the tailpiece and snap or clamp it onto the mounting plate. It should sit flat and square.

Step 5: Insert Batteries and Pair

Install fresh batteries, download the manufacturer's app and follow the pairing steps. Most apps run a calibration that learns which direction locks your door.

Step 6: Test Repeatedly

Lock and unlock from the app, the physical key and the thumb turn on the smart unit, with the door open and then closed. Test auto-lock and auto-unlock features before relying on them.

How to Install an Adhesive Smart Lock

  1. Clean the area around the thumb turn with the alcohol wipe supplied.
  2. Fit the adapter that matches your thumb turn shape.
  3. Position the unit so the adapter engages the thumb turn and the base sits flat, then press firmly for 30 to 60 seconds.
  4. Wait the recommended time (often a few hours) for the adhesive to cure before heavy use.
  5. Pair with the app and calibrate.

Adding Keyless Entry From Outside

Retrofit and adhesive locks keep your exterior keyhole, so you unlock from outside with the app, auto-unlock or the key. If you want a code pad, most brands sell a wireless keypad that mounts beside the door with adhesive or two small screws into the trim. That gives you a code option without drilling the door itself.

What If Your Door Has No Deadbolt?

A smart deadbolt needs a bolt to turn. If your door only has a knob, your options are:

  • Drill for a deadbolt: the most secure route. Follow our deadbolt installation guide, then add any smart deadbolt.
  • Use a temporary or portable lock: good for renters and travel, but not a smart lock. See can you put a lock on a door without drilling.
  • Smart handle sets: some smart levers replace a knob using the same 2-1/8" hole, but they latch rather than deadbolt, so they offer less security on exterior doors.

Common Mistakes

  • Ignoring a sticky deadbolt. The motor will jam and drain batteries fast.
  • Buying for a double-cylinder deadbolt. These locks need an interior thumb turn.
  • Skipping auto-lock testing. Make sure you cannot be locked out when stepping outside briefly.
  • Using cheap batteries. Use the type the manufacturer specifies, usually alkaline or the supplied rechargeable pack.
  • Throwing away the original thumb turn. You need it if you move or the smart lock fails.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you install a smart lock without drilling?

Yes, if the door already has a deadbolt. Retrofit and adhesive smart locks fit over or replace only the interior thumb turn and need no drilling.

Are no-drill smart locks secure?

Retrofit locks keep your existing bolt and cylinder, so they are as secure as your current deadbolt. Make sure that deadbolt is a quality Grade 2 or better model with a strike secured by 3" screws.

Can renters install a smart lock?

Often yes, because retrofit locks are fully reversible. Check your lease and reinstall the original thumb turn when you move out.

Do no-drill smart locks work with my existing key?

Retrofit and adhesive models keep your exterior cylinder, so your key keeps working.

What happens if the batteries die?

The app warns you in advance. If they die, use your physical key from outside or the manual thumb turn on the smart unit from inside.

The Bottom Line

If your door has a working single-cylinder deadbolt, a retrofit smart lock is the cleanest no-drill upgrade: 15 minutes, a screwdriver, and you keep your keys. Renters can use adhesive models.
