Before You Start: Do the Key Test

Use your physical key to lock the deadbolt from outside. This one test splits the problem in half:

  • Key locks it smoothly: the mechanical parts are fine. The problem is power, electronics or a setting (Fixes 1, 2, 4, 5).
  • Key is stiff or won't turn fully: the bolt is binding or jammed. Fix the door or strike alignment first (Fix 3). No keypad setting will fix a mechanical jam.

Fix 1: No Response at All

  1. Replace the battery. The BE365 and FE595 use a single 9V battery behind the inside cover. Smart models (Connect, Encode) use four AA batteries. Use new alkaline batteries; rechargeables often run at a lower voltage.
  2. Check the keypad cable. Remove the inside cover and make sure the ribbon cable from the outside keypad is firmly connected and not pinched.
  3. Locked out with a dead battery? Use the key. Many Schlage keypad models also have 9V jump terminals so you can power the keypad briefly from outside.

Fix 2: Green Light Flashes but the Bolt Doesn't Move

Schlage's own support guidance for the BE365 points to a misaligned cam: the motor turns, but it isn't connected properly to the bolt. It often happens after a battery change or reinstall.

  1. Open the door.
  2. Remove the inside cover and unplug the keypad cable.
  3. Check that the deadbolt was installed with the bolt retracted. If the mounting bolts are covered by the cam, it is still misaligned.
  4. Reconnect the cable, then test several times with the door open before closing it.

Fix 3: Motor Strains or Bolt Stops Part-Way

A motorized bolt has far less force than your hand. If the bolt rubs on the strike plate, the motor stalls and gives up.

  • Test with the door open: lock with the keypad. If it locks fine with the door open but not closed, it's an alignment problem.
  • Check the strike hole: the bolt needs about 1-1/4" of depth to extend fully. File the strike opening or deepen the hole.
  • Check for door sag: tighten the top hinge screws, ideally with 3" screws into the stud.
  • Weatherstripping: very tight seals push the door away from the frame, so the bolt binds. Try locking while pulling the door in slightly. If that works, adjust the strike.

Fix 4: Red Lights

  • Repeated red flashes when pressing buttons: often a low battery. Replace it.
  • Red after several wrong codes: many models lock out the keypad briefly after repeated wrong entries. Wait and try again.
  • Code not accepted at all: the code may have been deleted or the lock reset. You may need to reprogram it.

Fix 5: Locks, Then Unlocks (Smart Models)

Schlage Connect, Sense and Encode deadbolts need to know which way your door locks (handing). If the handing calibration is wrong, the lock can drive the bolt the wrong way or report the wrong status. Run the handing or calibration step in the app or manual, and make sure auto-lock and app settings aren't fighting each other.

Fix 6: Keypad Lever Still Opens the Door

On FE595 and FE575 keypad levers, the outside lever is either locked or free. Pressing the Schlage button (on most models) locks the outside lever again after it has been unlocked by a code. If it won't lock, replace the battery and check the inside cable connection. If the lever is loose, the clutch may be worn and the lock may need service.

Still Not Locking?

  1. Try a factory reset, which erases codes. Only do this if you have the default codes from the sticker inside the lock or the user guide.
  2. If the key test showed a jammed bolt even with the door open, the deadbolt may be broken internally and need replacing.
  3. Contact Schlage support with your model number (on the inside of the lock or the box).
